A warm, charming, and delightful Single Page Application (SPA) designed to trigger the psychological joy and dopamine rush of ordering your favorite food—with **0 real calories**, **₹0 charged**, and pure serotonin!

Built with **React**, **Tailwind CSS**, **Leaflet.js / OpenStreetMap**, and **Web Audio API**.

## ✨ Features

- **Cozy Lifestyle Aesthetic:** Soft cream/warm butter backgrounds, vibrant warm coral buttons, pillowy shadows, and cheerful food badges.
- **Authentic Food Catalog:**
  - **Real Indian:** Hyderabadi Handi Dum Biryani, Old Delhi Butter Chicken & Garlic Naan, Shahi Paneer Butter Masala, Ghee Podi Masala Dosa, Warm Shahi Gulab Jamun.
  - **Street Food & Fast Food:** Double Smashed Cheeseburger, Loaded Truffle & Peri-Peri Crinkle Fries, Wood-Fired Truffle Margherita Pizza.
  - **Cozy Dinner & Bowls:** Creamy Wild Mushroom Truffle Fettuccine, Tokyo Salmon & Tuna Dragon Roll Platter.
- **Interactive Customizer Modal:**
  - Portion size selection (Regular, Feast, Jumbo).
  - Spice meter with cute icons (Mild 🌿, Medium 🌶️, Indian Desi Spicy 🔥, Firecracker 💥).
  - Add-ons with dynamic subtotal calculations.
  - Behavioral pickle dilemma toggle (*"Add them back, I changed my mind"*).
  - Chef special instructions.
- **Cart & Happy Hour Voucher (-100%):**
  - Itemized breakdown (Item total, packaging fee, platform fee, taxes).
  - Auto-applied `CRAVINGLY-HAPPY-HOUR` coupon dynamically striking down the total to **₹0.00**.
- **Interactive Live Map Tracking (Leaflet.js):**
  - Real OpenStreetMap with Carto Voyager clean tiles.
  - Real urban route from Koramangala 4th Block to Indiranagar 100ft Road in Bengaluru.
  - Electric scooter driver Ramesh navigating along real street coordinates.
  - Fast-forward speed controller (2x / 5x) and a funny "Call Rider" easter egg.
- **Joyful Arrival Climax:**
  - Native dual-tone Web Audio doorbell chime (*Ding-Dong: 659Hz -> 523Hz*).
  - Celebratory confetti pop.
  - Animated savings scorecard tracking money saved and calories dodged in `localStorage`.
